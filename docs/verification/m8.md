# M8 verification report

Claim-level verification and final abstention safety.

**`verified=true` is constructed in exactly one place**: a `VerifiedAnswer`, built only behind a
PASS, after every material claim in the draft survived deterministic citation, provenance, numeric,
unit, negation and certainty checks, then semantic support verification, then contradiction
checking. M7 drafts remain `UNVERIFIED_AWAITING_CLAIM_VERIFICATION`. `answering_enabled` stays
`Literal[False]`: releasing a verified answer object is not the user-facing Ask experience, which
is M9 and remains disabled.

**Every number below was measured over synthetic content written for this repository.** No
clinician reviewed it and none of it is evidence about clinical performance. Where this report says
**synthetic engineering benchmark**, that is the complete claim.

## 3. Claim extraction

Deterministic (`claim-extraction-m8-v1`) over `draft.answer`, **not** over the generator's declared
claim list. Declared claims supply citations to the propositions they cover; a material proposition
matching none keeps an empty citation list and fails `CLAIM_NOT_CITED`.

This is the milestone's most important structural decision. A generator can write a sentence and
omit it from its own claim list, and every later stage would then verify a subset of what a reader
sees. `test_answer_prose_the_generator_never_declared_is_extracted_and_uncited` drives exactly that
draft and asserts the smuggled sentence is extracted, uncited and failed.

Splitting is biased toward over-splitting: an extra fragment costs one verification, while a
proposition welded to a supported one is how "Drug A treats B **and is safe in pregnancy**" passes
whole. Decimals and abbreviations are protected from sentence splitting. Connective fragments are
non-material and never block an answer.

## 4. Deterministic checks, which bind

Run before any model, and no model verdict can overturn one.
`test_a_wrong_dose_is_caught_without_asking_a_model` asserts the verifier recorded **zero calls**
while returning `SUPPORTED` for a 50 mg claim against 5 mg evidence.

| Check | Failure codes |
|---|---|
| Citation identity in *this* EvidenceSet | `UNKNOWN_CITATION` |
| Provenance resolves (document, version, parse run, pages, elements, spans) | `PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED` |
| Material claim carries a citation | `CLAIM_NOT_CITED` |
| `(value, normalized unit)` agreement | `NUMERIC_MISMATCH`, `UNIT_MISMATCH` |
| Polarity agreement with passages about the subject | `NEGATION_REVERSED` |
| Claim no more certain than its evidence | `OVERSTATED_CERTAINTY` |
| Cited table carries header rows; formula artifact present | `PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED` |
| Claim rests on a figure | `VISUAL_INTERPRETATION_REQUIRED` |

Unit aliases are normalized (`milligrams` → `mg`), so equivalent spellings pass while 5 mg versus
5 mL fails as a unit mismatch rather than being silently accepted.

## 5. Semantic verification

`ClaimVerifier` receives one claim and only the evidence that claim cites — asserted by
`test_the_verifier_receives_only_the_claim_and_its_cited_evidence`. No corpus handle, no retrieval,
no web search, no rank and no score reach it. Verdicts: `SUPPORTED`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED`,
`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `UNVERIFIABLE`. Output is a strict schema; prose is recorded for a reviewer
and never used as the decision. A verdict naming unsupplied evidence raises
`VERIFIER_UNKNOWN_EVIDENCE`; a provider failure raises `VERIFIER_FAILED`; malformed output raises
`VERIFIER_MALFORMED_OUTPUT`. Every one abstains — `test_a_missing_verifier_fails_closed_rather_than_passing`
covers the case where no verifier exists at all.

`ModelClaimVerifier` reaches a provider through the same `LLMProvider` protocol as the generator, so
no new vendor surface exists; a test scans `backend/app/verification` for vendor endpoints and
imports and finds none.

## 6. Generator/verifier independence

| Configuration | Result |
|---|---|
| `MEDRAG_VERIFIER__*` set and different from the generator | `independent_of_generator: true` |
| Unset — generator's model reused | `independent_of_generator: false`, warned in the inspector |
| Neither generator nor verifier configured | `VERIFIER_FAILED`; nothing is released |

**In this environment the verifier is the same model as the generator** (`gpt-5.6-sol`), because
`MEDRAG_VERIFIER__*` is not configured. That is a real weakness: a verifier sharing the generator's
blind spots is least likely to fail the claim the generator was most confident about. It is
permitted rather than enforced so M8 does not require two paid accounts, and it is recorded in
every `VerifierSpec` rather than glossed over.

## 7. Contradiction

Beyond M7's block-to-block comparison, which its own report records as missing prose-level
contradictions: a claim supported by its cited source and contradicted by a source retrieval
**retained but did not cite** — the shape a generator creates by citing only what agrees with it;
two reference-grade sources stating different values in the same unit; and assessment-only support
standing against a reference source. One document stating several values for one unit is a range,
not a disagreement.

Competing evidence is preserved and returned. Rank never breaks a tie — asserted by scanning
`contradiction.py` for `reranker_score`, `fused_score`, `reranked_rank` and `fused_rank`, none of
which appear.

## 8. Final policy and repair

`PASS` requires every material claim `SUPPORTED`, deterministic checks clean, no contradiction.
`REGENERATE_ONCE` only when **every** failure is repairable. `ABSTAIN` otherwise — including any
contradiction, any unresolvable citation and any verifier failure.

Repair is capped at one by type (`max_attempts: Literal[1]`), receives the same evidence and never
more (`may_widen_evidence: Literal[False]`), is told which statements failed and why, and is
instructed that removing an unsupported statement is success. The repaired draft runs the entire
flow again. `test_a_failed_repair_abstains_without_a_second_attempt` asserts exactly two
generations and a `REPAIR_FAILED` abstention.

## 10. Three real defects the evaluation found

The first evaluation run produced **two false PASSes and one unnecessary abstention**. All three
were genuine defects, fixed rather than relabelled:

**A numeric claim drawn from a table skipped the table check entirely.** `classify()` returns a
single type and tests `NUMERIC` before `TABLE_DERIVED`, so a claim quoting a number from a table
was `NUMERIC` and the header-row branch never ran — a table part with no headers passed.
`check_structured_evidence` now inspects the blocks the claim **cites** rather than its type label.

**`check_negation` fired on a claim containing no negation at all.** For structured evidence
(a table rendered as short fragments) no single sentence met the overlap bar, and the fallback
reported reversal rather than agreement. It now reports only a genuine polarity disagreement: some
passage about the subject must carry the opposite polarity and none may carry the claim's.

**The question-bank conflict fixture did not encode the scenario it named** — its key and its
reference block were not lexically about the same subject, so no detector could have related them.
The fixture was corrected to state the same quantity. The underlying limitation is real and is
recorded in §13: lexically distant contradictions are not detected.

## 13. Known limitations

- **Synthetic engineering benchmark, not clinical validation.** 19 verification cases, written
  alongside the checks they measure.
- **The verifier is currently the same model as the generator**, so a shared blind spot can pass
  both stages. Configure `MEDRAG_VERIFIER__*` for genuine independence.
- Deterministic checks are lexical. A contradiction expressed in non-overlapping words is not
  detected, and a shared label with a shared unit can occasionally fire a false conflict.
- Claim extraction is rule-based. Unusual punctuation, lists and non-English text will decompose
  imperfectly; over-splitting is the intended failure direction.
- Certainty and negation vocabularies are fixed word lists, not a grammar. Negation scope is
  approximated by sentence-level polarity.
- Visually dependent claims always abstain. Correct today, still a coverage gap.
- Abstention rate on the fixture is 0.79 and one repair is the hard cap. Coverage is deliberately
  traded for safety.
- No calibrated confidence exists anywhere, by design.
- **M9 has not started.** No conversations, no citations UI, no source-page highlighting, no
  streaming, and the production Ask experience is disabled.
